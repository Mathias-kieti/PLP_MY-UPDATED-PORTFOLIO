Privacy
policy.

Effective date: 24 November 2026

This policy explains how KITO, a WhatsApp-based AI assistant created and managed by Mathias Kieti (“we”, “us”), handles information when you message it. KITO is an AI assistant; it is not Mathias and does not represent that it is a human.

1. Information we process

When you interact with KITO, the service may process:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and sender identifier.
  • The messages, attachments or other content you choose to send, to the extent supported and received by the service.
  • Messages generated by KITO, conversation context and information needed to maintain continuity between messages.
  • Message identifiers, timestamps, delivery or status information, and technical or workflow logs used to operate and troubleshoot the service.

Please avoid sending passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information unless it is genuinely necessary. Do not use KITO for emergencies.

2. How information is used

Information is used to receive and respond to your messages, generate AI-assisted replies, maintain relevant conversation context, operate the WhatsApp integration, troubleshoot errors, and identify messages that should be passed to Mathias for personal attention.

If you ask to speak with Mathias or request that a message be passed to him, relevant message content and sender details may be forwarded to him through the configured notification channel.

3. Service providers and sharing

KITO relies on third-party services to function. Depending on the message and workflow, information may be processed by:

  • Meta / WhatsApp: to send and receive WhatsApp messages and related delivery events.
  • Groq: to process prompts and generate AI responses.
  • Cloudflare: to provide secure network connectivity to the hosted automation service and related request handling.
  • Hosting and database infrastructure: to run the automation workflow and store conversation context, including the configured PostgreSQL database.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy documentation. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or where necessary to protect the service, its users, or its operator. We do not sell personal information or use message content to serve advertising.

4. Storage and retention

Conversation context is stored in the service's database so KITO can respond with relevant context in later messages. Operational records may also be retained in the automation platform or by service providers.

There is currently no fixed automatic deletion period configured for all conversation data. Information may remain stored until it is deleted, the relevant storage is cleared, or it is no longer needed for the purposes described above. Retention periods at third-party providers are governed by their own policies and settings.

5. Your choices and privacy requests

You can stop interacting with KITO at any time. You may also request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with your WhatsApp number, subject to applicable legal requirements and technical limitations.

To make a privacy request, email mathiasnzioka0@gmail.com with the subject “KITO Privacy Request”. Include enough information for us to identify the relevant conversation, but do not send passwords or other sensitive credentials. We may need to verify that you control the WhatsApp number before acting on a request.

6. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect the service and the information it handles. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children

KITO is a general-purpose assistant and is not specifically designed for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to KITO, contact us using the details below so the concern can be reviewed.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as KITO's features, providers or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page indicates when it was most recently revised. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
